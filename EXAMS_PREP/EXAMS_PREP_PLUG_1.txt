Therefore, if two integers a and b are congruent modulo M, it means they have the same remainder
under modulo m. You can also state it this way: Two INTEGERS a and b, are said to be congruent modulo M, if their difference (i.e., a-b), is evenly divisible
by M. This equivalence relation is compatible with the operations of addition, subtraction, and multiplicati
