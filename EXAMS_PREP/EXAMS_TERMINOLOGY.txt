The System is the ensambly of people, organisation, application, hardware, clients, user, product, all are part of the system, that within the whole systems each component describe erlier are sub-system by its own.
A subject can be a physical person.
A person can also be a legal firm.
Principal can be a person or an equipment, or the card, or a deputy, or a complex role.
Secrecy is a technical term – mechanisms limiting the number of principals who can access information
Privacy means control of your own secrets
Confidentiality is an obligation to protect someone else’s secrets 
Anonymity from not being able to identify subjects to not being able to link their actions
Object’s integrity lies in not having been altered since the last authorized modification
Authentication has two common means an object has integrity plus freshness or You are speaking to the right principal 
A trusted system or component is one that can break my security policy. I am going to use Number 2 – the defense industry definition. A GCHQ person selling keys to the Russians is trusted but not trustworthy. (Let’s assume their actions are unauthorized).
A security policy is a succinct statement of protection goals – typically less than a page of a normal language
"Succinct" means to express something clearly and briefly, using as few words as possible without losing the core meaning
A protection profile is a detailed statement of protection goals – typically dozens of pages of semi-formal language 
A security target is a detailed statement of protection goals applied to a particular system – and may be hundreds of pages of specification for both functionality and testing. 
A cybersecurity policy is a formal document that defines the rules, expectations, and overall strategy an organization uses to protect its digital assets, data, and systems from unauthorized access and cyber threats
