Therefore, if two integers a and b are congruent modulo M, it means they have the same remainder
under modulo m. You can also state it this way: Two INTEGERS a and b, are said to be congruent modulo M, if their difference (i.e., a-b), is evenly divisible
by M. This equivalence relation is compatible with the operations of addition, subtraction, and multiplicati
For example, let us illustrate using this congruence modulo function, 6 is congruent modulo 2 (mod 4): 6 ≡ 2 (mod 4)

Furthermore, a ≡ b (mod m) is true, if and only if there is an integer k such that a = b + km.

Property
1. b and c are equivalent modulo m, if and only if (b − c)/m is an integer. The number m is called the modulus, 
  and the statement "b is congruent to c (modulo m)" is written mathematically as
  b ≡ c (mod m)
  Another way to present this, b ≡ c( mod m) (by the definition of congruence) is m|(b − c). m|(b − c) is read as m "evenly divides” in b-c.
  e.g.:(15 - 3)/12 this equal to 1
2. Let b and c be integers, and let m be a positive integer. Then b ≡ c (mod m) if and only if b mod m = c mod m.

Digital Forensic Science
Forensic Science & Digital Forensics

Using a cybersecurity context, you could define them as:

Forensic Science (Criminalistics):
The application of scientific methods and techniques to collect, analyze, and present evidence for legal 
proceedings involving criminal or civil cases. In cybersecurity, forensic principles help ensure that 
digital evidence is handled in a manner that preserves its integrity and admissibility in court.

Digital Forensics:
A specialized branch of forensic science focused on identifying, preserving, analyzing, and presenting 
evidence from digital devices and information systems. In cybersecurity, digital forensics is used to 
investigate cyber incidents, data breaches, malware infections, unauthorized access, and other 
computer-related crimes while maintaining the integrity of digital evidence.

The PEB (Process Environment Block) is accessible from user mode and can be modified by an attacker or malware. 
In contrast, the VAD (Virtual Address Descriptor) is maintained by the operating system within kernel memory 
and is not easily accessible or altered by user-mode processes. Therefore, while malware may manipulate information 
stored in the PEB, the VAD often retains the correct memory mapping records, making it a more reliable source during 
forensic analysis.

Challenges of Digital evidence
• It is in digital form therefore not easily intelligible
• It is volatile
• It is fragile – can be altered; manipulated; may change forms
• Can be duplicated without degradation
• May be available in different jurisdictions
• May requires special tools, and procedures to examine & investigate
• Admissibility – must be obtained in compliance with legislation or best practice to be admissible in court
